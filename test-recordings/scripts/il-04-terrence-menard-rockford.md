# Terrence, Rockford: 22 years at Menard, registration deadline and host-site rules

**Mode:** New intake · **Meeting:** In-person visit · **Date:** November 20, 2026 · **Set agency location to:** Rockford, Illinois

Every person and phone number here is made up.

## Why this case

People convicted of sex offenses in Illinois often cannot find an approved host site and may serve MSR inside prison ('violated at the door'). Once out, they must register with local police within days and follow restrictions on where they live and go online. These conditions are sensitive but must be captured accurately.

Sources: <https://www.thejha.org/s/JHA-Statement-on-Housing-for-IDOC-Populations-May-2020.pdf>, <https://idoc.illinois.gov/content/dam/soi/en/web/idoc/reportsandstatistics/documents/Loyolatechnicalviolatorreport2023.pdf>, <https://idoc.illinois.gov/parole/conditionsofsupervision.html>
