# Gloria, Springfield: 64, insulin, a wheelchair, and her daughter on speakerphone

**Mode:** New intake · **Meeting:** In-person visit · **Date:** November 17, 2026 · **Set agency location to:** Springfield, Illinois

Every person and phone number here is made up.

## Why this case

Older adults leaving Illinois prisons need Medicare, Medicaid, durable medical equipment and accessible housing. Decatur Correctional Center holds women. Family members often join by phone, and older clients give vague dates.

Sources: <https://hfs.illinois.gov/content/dam/soi/en/web/hfs/sitecollectiondocuments/hfsreentryimplementationplan.pdf>

## Script

**Case manager:** Intake for Gloria Watts, Tuesday November seventeenth. Is it okay to record this?

**Gloria:** Go ahead, honey.

**Case manager:** And your daughter is on the phone with us?

**Daughter (speakerphone):** Yes, this is Monique. I'm in St. Louis.

**Case manager:** Gloria, when did you come home?

**Gloria:** Oh, it was around Halloween. A day or two before.

**Daughter (speakerphone):** It was the twenty-ninth, Mama. I picked you up in Decatur.

**Gloria:** That's right, the twenty-ninth.

**Case manager:** And are you on MSR?

**Gloria:** Two years. My agent's a young man, I don't remember his name.

**Case manager:** How old are you?

**Gloria:** Sixty-four.

**Case manager:** Tell me about your health.

**Gloria:** Sugar. I take insulin, the Lantus, twenty units at night. And I'm in this wheelchair since my stroke. The chair is the prison's, they want it back.

**Case manager:** How much insulin do you have?

**Gloria:** Maybe a week.

**Daughter (speakerphone):** She has two pens left, it's less than that.

**Case manager:** Where are you staying?

**Gloria:** At my friend's on the east side, but there's three steps and no ramp.

**Case manager:** Do you have Medicare?

**Gloria:** I think so. I got a red white and blue card a long time ago.

**Case manager:** Okay. Today I'll call SIU Medicine's clinic for an insulin refill this week, and I'll start a wheelchair request through Medicaid. Monique, can I call you if I can't reach your mom?

**Daughter (speakerphone):** Yes, three one four, five five five, zero one six two.

## What it probes

- Vague date ('around Halloween, a day or two before') corrected by the daughter; client agrees.
- Medication supply disagreement: client says a week, daughter says less (two pens).
- Durable medical equipment that will be taken back (wheelchair belongs to IDOC).
- Accessible housing barrier (three steps, no ramp).
- Daughter's out-of-state phone becomes the emergency contact, not the client's number.
- Supervision officer name not known.
