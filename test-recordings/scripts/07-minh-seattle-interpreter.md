# Minh, Seattle: Vietnamese with an interpreter and an immigration hold worry

**Mode:** New intake · **Meeting:** In-person visit · **Date:** November 17, 2026 · **Set agency location to:** Seattle, Washington

## Why this case

Non-citizens released from state prison may be transferred to ICE or fear it; many need interpreters. Three-way conversations through an interpreter make it hard to tell who said what, and Whisper may transcribe or translate the non-English parts.

Sources: <https://www.cdcr.ca.gov/ccjbh/wp-content/uploads/sites/172/2021/08/SB-369-Barriers-Report_Final-ADA.pdf>

## What it probes

- Three speakers, two languages. The interpreter speaks for the client ('He says yes').
- Vietnamese name order (family name first: Tran Van Minh). The note must not reorder or 'correct' it.
- Whisper may output Vietnamese text, an English translation, or nonsense for the Vietnamese lines.
- Interpreter adds their own words ('He's worried...') that the client's Vietnamese supports only partly.
- Immigration status: sensitive; 'green card' and deportation fear.
- Washington terms: 'community custody' (= supervision), Monroe Correctional Complex, Rainier Beach.
