# Nadia, Toronto: Canadian terms, French words, and corrections that should win

**Mode:** New intake · **Meeting:** In-person visit · **Date:** November 25, 2026 · **Set agency location to:** Toronto, Ontario

Every person and phone number here is made up.

## Why this case

In Ontario people leave provincial jails (sentences under two years) on probation, need a new OHIP health card, and apply for Ontario Works instead of SNAP. Case managers often read details back and clients correct them.

Sources: <https://www.ncsl.org/research/civil-and-criminal-justice/providing-identification-for-those-released-from-incarceration.aspx>

## What it probes

- The case manager states a wrong age and place; the client corrects both. The correction must win.
- Canadian programs and IDs: OHIP, SIN, Ontario Works, ServiceOntario. Mapping to the US-style categories (Food = SNAP is wrong here).
- French filler and words (oui, euh, parfait).
- 'Vanier, in Milton' = Vanier Centre for Women. 'Kennedy station' (TTC).
- Probation conditions that include a no-contact order (sensitive, third party).
