## Why this case

Overdose risk is highest in the first two weeks after release, and people started on medication for opioid use disorder (MOUD) inside often face a gap. Older people coming home after long sentences carry many prescriptions and are often veterans.

Sources: <https://www.nih.gov/news-events/news-releases/treating-opioid-addiction-jails-improves-treatment-engagement-reduces-overdose-deaths-reincarceration>, <https://evidence2impact.psu.edu/resources/preventing-overdose-deaths-during-high-risk-transition-periods>
