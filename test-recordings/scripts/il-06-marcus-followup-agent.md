# Marcus follow-up call: the agent's visit, a lease problem and a threatened violation

**Mode:** Existing-client update · **Meeting:** Phone call · **Date:** November 24, 2026 · **Set agency location to:** Chicago, Illinois

Every person and phone number here is made up.

## Why this case

A host site can be lost when the leaseholder's landlord objects, and losing it can lead to a technical violation and return to prison. Follow-ups mix good news, bad news and relatives' comments.

Sources: <https://idoc.illinois.gov/content/dam/soi/en/web/idoc/reportsandstatistics/documents/Loyolatechnicalviolatorreport2023.pdf>, <https://www.thejha.org/s/JHA-Statement-on-Housing-for-IDOC-Populations-May-2020.pdf>

## Script

**Case manager:** Hi Marcus, it's Denise, Tuesday the twenty-fourth. Quick check-in. Still okay to record?

**Case manager:** Did the LINK card come?

**Marcus:** It came Saturday. Two ninety-eight on it.

**Case manager:** Great. And the ID?

**Marcus:** Fixed. New address and everything.

**Case manager:** And the phone?

**Marcus:** That's this phone. Lifeline came yesterday. The number is three one two, five five five, zero one nine one.

**Case manager:** Good. Anything else?

**Marcus:** My sister's landlord found out I'm there. He says I gotta go by the end of the month or he'll file on her.

**Sister (background):** Tell her the agent said he'll violate you if you move without telling him. *[talks over the last speaker, in the background]*

**Marcus:** Yeah. Agent Dominguez said if I move without a new host site approved, that's a violation.

**Case manager:** Okay. That's serious. Today I'll call St. Leonard's and the Safer Foundation for a residence that IDOC approves as a host site, and I'll call Agent Dominguez myself on Thursday. Call me Friday.

**Marcus:** Thursday's Thanksgiving though.

**Case manager:** You're right. I'll call him Wednesday then. Call me Friday.

**Marcus:** Okay.

- A new phone number for the client (a change to contact).
- Housing threat with a deadline ('end of the month' = Nov 30) and a violation risk.
- A background relative relays what the agent said; the client then confirms it himself.
- A self-corrected next step: Thursday is Thanksgiving (Nov 26), so the call moves to Wednesday.
